Eduard Zamfir

I am a PhD student in Computer Science at the University of Würzburg, advised by Radu Timofte and affiliated with the ELLIS Unit Franconia. I am fortunate to closely collaborate with Yulun Zhang, Danda Pani Paudel, and Luc van Gool.

I received my M.Sc. in Computational Engineering from TU Darmstadt, where I was a research assistant in the Visual Inference Lab under Stefan Roth, and my B.Sc. in Mechanical Engineering with a focus on mechatronics and automotive engineering, also from TU Darmstadt.
